# Audio Attributions

## Songs (`res/raw/song_*.mp3`)

All four songs are from **"Sing Out Loud Children's Songs"**, published by the
U.S. Department of State, American English (americanenglish.state.gov) for free
educational use. As works published by the U.S. federal government they are in
the **public domain** (17 U.S.C. § 105).

All 13 songs of the collection are bundled (`song_alphabet`, `song_one_two`,
`song_twinkle`, `song_bingo`, `song_head_shoulders`, `song_hokey`, `song_happy`,
`song_hickory`, `song_mary_lamb`, `song_sleeping`, `song_teapot`,
`song_river_woods`, `song_hush_baby`), re-encoded to mono 96 kbps MP3 for size.

Source: https://americanenglish.state.gov/resources/sing-out-loud-childrens-songs
